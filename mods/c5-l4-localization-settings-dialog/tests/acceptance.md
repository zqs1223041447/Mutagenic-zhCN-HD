# C5-L4 acceptance scope

Machine checks:

- dependency chain resolves against the original fingerprint;
- all three raw preimages match and apply exactly once;
- resource structural tokens remain unchanged;
- the physical pack delta is explained by the resolved manifest;
- font coverage is available for the new Chinese text;
- PCK listing, checksum normalization, PE embedding, full extraction, and
  extracted-tree comparison pass;
- the exact candidate boots without an `ALERT` modal.

Phase checkpoint:

- launch the exact SHA-bound candidate with its adjacent DLL;
- inspect the Settings dialog title and the two navigation labels;
- check glyphs, fallback, clipping, layout, and return responsiveness.

The phase checkpoint is retained as evidence and is not an active blocker for
independent machine-verifiable work.
